# Ghost Hub SDK Sales Playbook

## Target Accounts

### Tier 1: Enterprise IoT Companies
- **Characteristics**: 1000+ employees, IoT products, need AI integration
- **Pain Points**: Siloed systems, manual workflows, integration complexity
- **Use Cases**: Smart manufacturing, connected products, automation

### Tier 2: SaaS Platforms
- **Characteristics**: 100-1000 employees, workflow automation needs
- **Pain Points**: Scaling workflows, multi-tenant architecture, AI features
- **Use Cases**: Workflow automation, process orchestration, AI assistants

### Tier 3: System Integrators
- **Characteristics**: Consulting firms, implementation partners
- **Pain Points**: Reusable frameworks, fast delivery, standards compliance
- **Use Cases**: Client implementations, custom solutions, training

---

## Discovery Questions

### Business Impact
1. What workflows are currently manual or inefficient?
2. How much time is spent on workflow orchestration?
3. What would be the impact of 10x faster workflow development?
4. What are your Q4/Q1 business priorities?

### Technical Requirements
1. What IoT protocols do you use? (MQTT, HTTP, CoAP)
2. How many IoT devices need integration?
3. Do you have existing AI agents or frameworks?
4. What are your deployment constraints? (cloud, on-prem, hybrid)

### Organization
1. Who owns workflow automation?
2. Who are the technical decision-makers?
3. What's your approval process for new tools?
4. Who would be the project champion?

## Objection Handling

### "We already use [competitor]"
**Response**: "What specific gaps have you experienced? Ghost Hub is API-first and can complement existing tools. Many customers use us alongside their existing workflow tools for AI-specific use cases."

### "The price is too high"
**Response**: "Let me show you the ROI calculator. Customers typically see 5-10x return within 90 days through reduced development time and increased automation capability."

### "We need custom features"
**Response**: "Enterprise includes custom development and dedicated support. Let me connect you with our solutions team to discuss your specific requirements."

### "We'll need to evaluate"
**Response**: "I recommend starting with the Community tier to evaluate. Once you're ready to expand, we offer seamless upgrade paths and migration support."
